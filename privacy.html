Privacy policy

friday-openclaw is a personal, single-user application. The person who operates it is the only person whose Google data it touches.

What data is accessed

With the operator's consent, the application accesses the operator's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts through Google's APIs, to carry out tasks the operator asks their assistant to perform.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored on a server owned and controlled by the operator. Content retrieved from Google is processed to fulfil the operator's request and is not copied to any third-party service or shared with anyone.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any other party.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access, or by deleting the stored token on the operator's server.
