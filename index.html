friday-openclaw

friday-openclaw is the Google OAuth client for a personal AI assistant ("friday", running on OpenClaw) operated by a single individual for their own use.

It lets that assistant read and manage the operator's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts on the operator's behalf, on hardware the operator owns.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no other users, and nothing to install.
